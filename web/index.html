    About the numbers
    
YN00 and NG86 reproduce PAML yn00 (weighting = 0). ML reproduces PAML codeml with
    runmode = -2, model = 0: t, kappa and omega by maximum likelihood with standard errors from the
    curvature of the likelihood. Validated pair by pair against PAML 4.10 on PAML's example data under eleven genetic codes.
    With more than two sequences every pair is estimated independently; there is no tree-based estimate.
    Source and command-line tool: github.com/hyphaltip/subopt-kaks.

    

    
Visits, and each press of Run (a count only, never the sequences), are counted anonymously with
    GoatCounter: no cookies and no personal data, just the page address, the
    referring site and the browser and screen size.
